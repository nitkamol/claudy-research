Macro Regime Fit: Current regime is Reflation (Rising Growth + Rising Inflation) with a US industrial policy overlay. Semiconductors are bifurcated: AI/data center chips are in a structural growth supercycle (tailwind), while legacy PC/client chips face secular ARM displacement pressure (headwind). Intel straddles both worlds — and its foundry pivot adds a third dimension: US sovereign manufacturing policy play, aligned with CHIPS Act and geopolitical de-risking from Taiwan. In a Reflation regime, industrial capex stories with government backing benefit from multiple expansion; however, rising real yields compress long-duration growth narratives at extreme multiples.

Business Cycle Position: Late-cycle globally, but Intel specifically is at early-innings corporate turnaround — the company bottomed in 2023-2024 and is now in Phase 1 of its foundry inflection. This creates an unusual dynamic: company cycle (early recovery) vs macro cycle (late expansion).

Transmission Mechanism: Apple chip manufacturing deal + Google multi-gen CPU commitment → external foundry credibility established → Intel Foundry re-rated from "loss-center" to "strategic asset" → equity multiple expansion from 18x to 25-35x if 18A yield delivers → but rising real yields cap how far that re-rating can extend at current price levels.

Investment Thesis: Intel is executing the most ambitious industrial pivot in semiconductor history — transforming from an integrated device manufacturer into a US-sovereign foundry for premium hyperscaler and consumer tech customers. Q1 2026 showed real recovery: DCAI +22%, revenue +7.4% YoY, massive EPS beat. The story is binary: 18A delivers commercially (bull: $150-200) or yield execution falters (bear: $50-60). At $116.63, the market has already priced in substantial delivery probability.

Portfolio Positioning: Satellite (3-10%) — too speculative for Core given unproven foundry execution; too potentially transformational to ignore entirely. Entry timing matters critically.
