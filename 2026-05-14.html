SECTION 2 — SENTIMENT-ADJUSTED CONVICTION

Peter's Sentiment Cycle places us in Anxiety-to-Denial, which is a specific and important position. Let me explain what this means for how we size our trades.

In the Euphoria phase, sentiment is your enemy — everyone is already long, returns are front-loaded, and you should reduce position sizes and add hedges because the risk-reward has deteriorated. In the Panic/Depression phase, sentiment is your ally — fear is overdone, quality assets are cheap, and you increase conviction on the strongest names because the margin of safety is highest.

Anxiety-to-Denial is neither. It is the transitional phase where the thesis is correct but timing is uncertain, the crowd is partially positioned (not fully), and the risk is a sudden acceleration into panic without a clean entry point if you wait too long. The practical implication:

  • Position sizes: 65–75% of maximum. Not full conviction. The thesis is right but the market has not confirmed it, and a geopolitical resolution (Ada Card 2) could temporarily invalidate the trade.
